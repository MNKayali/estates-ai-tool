Add it to Gmail
  1. Click Copy signature above. (If that does nothing, drag to select everything in the white box above, logo included, and press Ctrl+C.)
  2. In Gmail (mail.projento@gmail.com): ⚙ → See all settings → General, scroll to Signature, click + Create new and name it Projento.
  3. Click in the signature box and press Ctrl+V.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose the info@projento.co.uk address and set Projento for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
