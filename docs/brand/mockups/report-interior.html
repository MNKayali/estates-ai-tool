Executive Summary

This feasibility study examines a new six-storey speculative office development of 4200 m² GIFA on a cleared City-fringe site in Clerkenwell, targeting BREEAM Excellent and WELL Building Standard v2 certification. The estimated total project cost is £24,301,000 to £37,968,000 (excluding VAT), significantly exceeding the stated budget of £18,000,000. The assessment is Grade C (Limited Confidence) due to automated sense-check warnings and estimated quantities. The most material risk is the substantial budget shortfall, which requires scope, specification, or budget revision before proceeding to Stage 2.

Key findings

  • The estimated total project cost of £24,301,000 to £37,968,000 (excluding VAT) is driven by high specification, dual sustainability certifications, and Inner London location factors.
  • No target completion date has been specified; the 128-week programme is achievable if design, planning and procurement proceed without delay.
  • The stated budget of £18,000,000 (incl. fees and VAT) is insufficient, falling approximately £11,161,000 short of the estimated lower range; scope, specification or budget must be revisited.
  • Contaminated land remediation is a known issue requiring Phase 1 and Phase 2 surveys before Stage 2 to quantify cost and programme impact.
  • Traditional Single Stage Tender is recommended for its compatibility with a fixed design, clear specification, and competitive cost certainty in a stable Inner London market.
Budget check: shortfall. The stated budget of £18,000,000 (incl. fees and VAT) is below the estimated gross range of £29,161,000 – £45,562,000, a shortfall of approximately £11,161,000 against even the lower end. Scope, specification or budget will need to be revisited.
