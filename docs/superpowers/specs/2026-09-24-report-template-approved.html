Executive Summary

This report assesses the feasibility of refurbishing Hargreaves Teaching Block, a three-storey 1960s teaching building at Example University in Birmingham (1,200 m² GIFA), to provide modern flexible teaching space with full systems replacement, upgraded fire safety and improved thermal performance. The estimated total project cost is £1,600,000 to £1,994,000 excluding VAT, graded A (High Confidence). The target completion date of 1 September 2028 is not achievable: the programme completes around 28 May 2029. The most material risk is confining works to vacation windows, which constrains the construction programme.

Key findings

  • The estimated total project cost is £1,600,000 to £1,994,000 excluding VAT, driven mainly by full mechanical and electrical replacement and new windows across the 1,200 m² building.
  • The programme runs 124 weeks including 9 weeks of float, completing around 28 May 2029, about 38 weeks after the 1 September 2028 target.
  • Confining works to vacation windows is the most material risk and the main reason the programme spans several academic years.
  • The air source heat pump needs an electrical capacity and DNO check at Stage 2 before the heating design can be confirmed.
  • Conservation-area status means external alterations, including new windows, are likely to need full planning consent.
Budget check: sufficient. The stated budget of £4,500,000 (incl. fees and VAT) covers the estimated gross range of £1,920,000 – £2,393,000, with about £2,107,000 of headroom.

How the percentages were set

  • Preliminaries 10%: 8% base, plus 1% for partial occupation, 0.5% for restricted hours and 0.5% for a programme over 18 months.
  • Overheads and profit 9.5%: the standard band for works between £1m and £5m.
  • Professional fees 16%: 13.5% for a project at Stage 0–1, plus 1.5% for M&E-heavy scope and 1% for BREEAM.
  • Risk 8%: 5% baseline, plus 1.5% for asbestos, 0.5% for ageing services and 1% for a fixed completion date.
  • Contingency 5%: fixed for every report; survey uncertainty sits in the risk allowance.
  • Inflation 7%: 6% for 96 weeks to tender and 1% for a 26-week construction period.

Cost assumptions

Procurement Recommendation

RouteTraditional, single-stage tender
ContractJCT Standard Building Contract 2024
Tender type · designSingle stage · client design team

A traditional single-stage tender suits this refurbishment: the scope is clear, the budget is defined and public funding rules require formal competition. Fixing the contractor's price at tender gives the cost certainty the client prioritises.

Commercial considerations

  • Confirm phasing and re-mobilisation costs at Stage 2; consider a framework agreement to hold rates across phases.
  • Explore early procurement of major plant (switchgear, air handling, heat pump) to shorten the critical path.
  • Price restricted hours and vacation-window working explicitly in the tender.

Recommendations and Next Steps

  1. Confirm the asbestos survey is a current refurbishment and demolition survey; if it is, reuse it to shorten the survey period and reduce the risk allowance.
  2. Agree examination blackout dates and available vacation windows for each construction phase with the estates and academic teams.
  3. Commission a Stage 2 electrical study to confirm available capacity and obtain DNO approval for the air source heat pump.
  4. Seek pre-application advice on external works (windows, doors, plant) to confirm the planning route and expected standards.
  5. Develop a phasing strategy at Stage 2 and present options for meeting the target date or agreeing an achievable one.
