# EX-9 — Four-storey hotel bedroom wing, Edinburgh Old Town

## Story
An independent hotel group owns a Georgian townhouse hotel just off the Royal Mile in Edinburgh. It wants to add a four-storey, 2,400 m² rear bedroom wing (48 en-suite bedrooms) on the site of a former garage court. The site is in the Old Town conservation area and the World Heritage Site, the rear wall is shared with a neighbouring listed tenement, and vehicle access is through a narrow close. The hotel will keep trading throughout. There is no fixed budget yet; the owners want the likely cost and the earliest possible opening to catch the 2029 Festival season.
