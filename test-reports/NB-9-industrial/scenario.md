# NB-9 — 12,500 m² logistics warehouse, Doncaster

## Story
A private logistics developer is building a speculative-to-let distribution warehouse near junction 3 of the M18 at Doncaster. The building is a single-storey steel portal frame, 12,500 m² GIFA including a two-storey 600 m² office block, with 12 m eaves, dock levellers, a 9,000 m² HGV yard and 120 car spaces. The ground is former colliery land with made ground and a high water table, so piled foundations and ground improvement are likely. The developer has no fixed budget yet and wants the likely cost and programme for its funding application; speed to market matters most.

## Independent benchmark (my estimate, before running the tool)
- Construction cost, Q3 2026, Yorkshire: **£950–£1,350/m²** GIFA for a big-box warehouse with offices, piling, yard and parking → **£11.9m–£16.9m**.
- Total incl. fees, contingency and VAT: about **£16m–£24m** (VAT is recoverable for a commercial developer).
- Construction: **35–45 weeks**. From Stage 3 incl. reserved matters and tender: **about 15–20 months**.
- Watch: "typical scope" for industrial adds internal walls, wall / ceiling finishes and heating across the whole 12,500 m² — a real warehouse has none of that over the racking area, so cost may be overstated.
