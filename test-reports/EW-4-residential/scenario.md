# EW-4 — Riverside Court estate grounds refresh

## Story
Riverside Court is a 1990s housing association estate on the edge of Exeter: three blocks of flats around a shared car park, bin stores and communal gardens. Residents have complained for two winters about a pooling, cracked car park surface, an undersized bin store that overflows on collection day, and unlit paths at the rear that feel unsafe after dark. The association wants the car park resurfaced and drained properly, a larger bin store, better lighting, secure cycle storage, and EV charging bays ahead of a fleet of new leaseholder cars, without closing the car park residents rely on daily. Capital funding is fixed and tight, and the works must be finished before spring inspections.
