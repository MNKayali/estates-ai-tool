# OM-9 — Food factory refurbishment and extension, Peterborough

## Story
A chilled-food manufacturer runs a 1980s factory in Peterborough and needs to refurbish 2,200 m² of production space to high-care hygiene standard and add an 800 m² single-storey extension for a new production line (3,000 m² total). Production must continue, so the work is in two phases with three weeks between them for line commissioning. Scope: new hygienic wall panels and resin floors, process drainage, refrigeration-grade cooling, new ventilation with positive pressure, compressed air, and a larger power supply. Budget £7.5m including fees and VAT; the retailer customer wants the new line running by mid-2028.
