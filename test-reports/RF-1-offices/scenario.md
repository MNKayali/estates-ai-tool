# RF-1 — Floor-by-floor refurbishment, Leeds city centre offices

## Story
A regional property company owns a 1960s multi-let office block in central Leeds. Two of the four floors are due for lease renewal next year and the agent has warned that the finishes, heating and lighting are now noticeably behind what prospective tenants expect — one floor already lost a viewing over "tired" décor. The board has agreed to refurbish all four floors on a rolling basis rather than lose more tenants, refreshing finishes and bringing the ageing heating, power and lighting up to a lettable standard, while keeping at least half the building trading throughout. A shared party wall with the adjoining bank building limits what can be done to the façade.
