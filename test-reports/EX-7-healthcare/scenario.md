# EX-7 — Single-storey GP surgery extension, Cardiff

## Story
A GP partnership in Llanishen, north Cardiff, has outgrown its 1970s single-storey surgery after a practice merger. The health board will fund a 180 m² single-storey extension on the staff car park to add four consulting rooms, two treatment rooms and an accessible WC. The surgery must stay open throughout, so works will run around a fully occupied building with patients arriving all day. The existing building has suspected asbestos in the ceiling tiles where the new link opens through, and the flat roof leaks. The health board has offered £850,000 including fees and VAT, and the partners are worried that will not stretch.
