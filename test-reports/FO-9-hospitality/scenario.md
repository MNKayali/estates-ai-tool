# FO-9 — Restaurant and bar fit-out in a listed bank, Belfast

## Story
A restaurant group has leased the ground floor and basement (380 m²) of a former Victorian bank in Belfast city centre and plans a 140-cover restaurant and cocktail bar with an open kitchen. Everything back to the shell is to be replaced: kitchen extract through the roof, new drainage with grease traps, cooling, bespoke bar joinery and finishes, new WCs. The building is listed and in a conservation area, so the extract route and signage need consent. The group wants to open before Christmas 2027 and has not set a budget — it wants the tool to tell it what to expect.
