# DM-8 — Clearance of twelve void houses, Liverpool

## Story
A housing association is clearing six pairs of void 1930s semi-detached houses (12 homes, about 1,100 m² GIFA in total) in Walton, Liverpool, ahead of a new-build scheme. The houses have been boarded up for two years, have some asbestos (artex, soffits, garage roofs), and the end pair is attached to an occupied neighbour's house. There is no budget set yet — the association wants a figure for its board paper. Speed and low cost matter most.
