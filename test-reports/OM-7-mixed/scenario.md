# OM-7 — Department store conversion to shops and flats, Sheffield

## Story
A regeneration developer has bought a vacant 1930s department store on Fargate, Sheffield (4,800 m² over four floors). The plan keeps retail on the ground floor and converts the three upper floors to 40 build-to-rent apartments, with a new lift and stair core, new windows in the upper façade, a new roof covering, and all-new services. The building has asbestos throughout, some concrete repairs, and is in a conservation area. The developer has £11.5m including fees and VAT and wants first lettings in 2029. It is a mixed project: part refurbishment, part alteration.
