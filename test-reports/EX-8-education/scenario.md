# EX-8 — Three-storey teaching block extension, University of Leeds

## Story
A Russell Group university in Leeds wants to extend its 1990s engineering building with a three-storey, 1,800 m² teaching and laboratory wing. The extension wraps an occupied building, so construction will be delivered in two phases to keep the main entrance and teaching labs running, with a short break between them over the summer exam period. The university design guide applies, and the estates board has set a £12m envelope including fees and VAT. The site sits over a known culverted beck and old mine workings are mapped nearby, so ground risk is a concern.
