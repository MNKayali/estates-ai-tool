# Review — NB-2-education

Verdict: Fail — the region is wrong (GL54 is South West, not West Midlands, and the report's own risk register admits the postcode did not match and defaulted), the executive summary and the budget-check box on the same page give two different shortfall figures, the Financial Case section the scenario expects is missing entirely, and a ground-conditions risk is missing.
