# NB-2 — Elmfield Primary SEND resource base

## Story
A county council's SEND capital team needs a new single-storey resource
base on the edge of Elmfield Primary School's playing field in a
Cotswolds market town conservation area, to meet growing demand for
specialist places ahead of September admissions. The school stays open
throughout, so term-time-only working matters, and a modular build is
being considered to hit the deadline. There are mature trees along the
boundary and some made ground from an old bonfire/compost area to check.
DfE High Needs grant funding is confirmed and reasonably generous, but
the council needs to know the design and accessibility ambitions for
sensory and changing facilities are affordable within it before Stage 2
design is signed off.
