# Review — OM-2-other

Verdict: Mostly accurate and internally consistent (scope, risks, target-date check and financial-case omission all correct), but the budget shows a comfortable surplus where the scenario intended a tight, grant-capped shortfall, and the "Other" building-use description never surfaces as a labelled field.

## Issues
| # | Severity | Category | Page | Report says | Expected (scenario) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Answers | 1 | Cover: "North East, DH1 · Other · 220 m² GIFA"; metadata block shows only "Project type Other or mixed / Specification Basic" | Cover/summary should show building use as "Other — 'Community hub — hall, library corner and café'" | The Q1.3 free-text description for "Other" building use never appears as a labelled field anywhere (not on the cover, not in the info grid alongside Project type/Specification). It is only loosely paraphrased inside the Executive Summary prose ("a community hub comprising a flexible hall, library corner and café"), so a reader skimming the cover/metadata never sees what "Other" actually means. |
| 2 | Medium | Budget | 2, 6–7 | "Budget check: sufficient… gross range of £196,000 – £311,000, with headroom of approximately £39,000" | Scenario: "deliberately capped the budget low… no other funding to fall back on" — a shortfall verdict | Total project cost works out to only ≈£740–1,177/m² excl. VAT (≈£891–1,414/m² incl. VAT) for a Basic-spec heritage conversion that includes asbestos removal, structural and damp repair to a listed bell-tower, and full M&E replacement. That is low for 2026 UK heritage/listed-building work even at Basic spec, and is the reason the report shows £39,000 of headroom instead of the shortfall the scenario intended. Worth checking whether the heritage/listed-building cost loading is fully captured (only a one-off 2% professional-fee uplift is applied — see the percentage build-up on p.7) rather than treating this as confirmed correct. |
| 3 | Low | Layout | 10–11 | Appendix A, Part 1 ends with the bare group header "7 · WORK TO EXISTING BUILDINGS" as its last line; its three line items (Repairs, Alterations, Damp treatment) start at the top of Part 2 | A group header shouldn't be orphaned alone at a page break | Minor typographic fault — contrast with the same appendix table in OM-1, where the Part 1/Part 2 split falls cleanly between groups rather than immediately after an orphaned heading. |
