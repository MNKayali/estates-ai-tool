# OM-2 — Old chapel to community hub conversion, Durham (DH1)

## Story
A small charitable trust has taken a 99-year lease on a disused
early-Victorian chapel on the edge of Durham, empty and unheated for over
five years, and wants to convert it into a community hub — a flexible
hall, a small library corner and a café counter — funded mostly by a
heritage grant that carries a hard spending deadline. The building is
listed and sits within the conservation area, so the trustees are nervous
about both cost and how long consents will take. There's known damp and
some structural movement in the bell-tower corner, and the trustees have
deliberately capped the budget low because the grant won't stretch further
and they have no other funding to fall back on.
