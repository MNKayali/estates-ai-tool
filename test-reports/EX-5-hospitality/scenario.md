# EX-5 — Gym and studio extension at a seafront leisure centre

## Story
A district council leisure trust wants to add a two-storey gym and fitness
studio extension to its 1980s seafront leisure centre in Plymouth, to meet
growing membership demand and stop members lapsing to a private gym down
the road. The extension sits alongside the existing pool hall and shares a
party wall with the adjoining community library, and the centre has to stay
fully open to members throughout construction — noisy work has to be
scheduled around swim session times. A comfortable capital allocation has
already been approved by the council, so the trust's priority is design
quality and pace rather than cost control, though they haven't yet settled
on the planning route and want pre-application advice first.
