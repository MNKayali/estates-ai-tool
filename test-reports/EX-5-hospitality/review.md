# Review — EX-5-hospitality

Verdict: Internally consistent (budget, region, risks, financial case all
foot correctly) but fails its own scenario expectation on budget — the
estimate comes out so high (£5,758/m² total project cost mid) that a budget
the scenario intended to be "comfortably within budget" instead shows a
£2.1m shortfall; worth checking the leisure-specific rates (showers, sports
flooring, toilets group) against real project examples. Read via
`pdftotext -enc UTF-8 -layout` (page-image rendering was unavailable); the
Order of Cost Estimate (p.7), Appendix A and Programme detail (p.5) tables
print with values misaligned relative to their row labels in the extracted
text (worse than the same tables in EX-4/EX-6, where the shift was a clean,
reconstructable one-row offset) — the total figures were confirmed by
back-calculating through the stated Grade D range width (−25%/+30%) and
matching the cover, Key Findings and Financial Case mid-points, but
individual Appendix A / Programme-detail line figures could not be
reliably reconstructed and are not asserted here. A visual/PDF-image check
of pages 5 and 7 is recommended.
