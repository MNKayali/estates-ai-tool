# DM-9 — Removal of a CLASP prefab classroom block, Chelmsford

## Story
A secondary school in Chelmsford has a single-storey 1960s CLASP system-built classroom block (420 m²) that is beyond economic repair and full of asbestos (AIB panels, cladding, floor tiles). The local authority wants it removed in the summer holiday of 2027 and the area grassed over. The block is 2 m from the occupied sports hall. Budget is £150,000 including fees and VAT.
