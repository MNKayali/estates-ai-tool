# EW-5 — The Pulteney Hotel courtyard and terrace renewal

## Story
The Pulteney is a 28-room boutique hotel in a Georgian terrace in central Bath, within the city's conservation area. Its rear courtyard car park and raised garden terrace — used for wedding drinks receptions and summer dining — have settled unevenly, the retaining wall to the terrace is bowing, and the planting is tired. Ahead of a refurbished restaurant launch, the owners want the courtyard repaved, the retaining wall rebuilt, new landscaping and lighting, and a small cycle store for staff, all sympathetic to the World Heritage Site setting. An architect has already taken the design to Stage 3, and full planning permission will be needed given the conservation area. The hotel trades throughout and access via the narrow rear lane must be maintained.
