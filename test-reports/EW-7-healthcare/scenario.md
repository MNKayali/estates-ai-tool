# EW-7 — Hospital staff car park extension, Nottingham

## Story
An NHS trust is extending a surface staff car park at its Nottingham city hospital onto 6,500 m² of former nursery gardens. The scheme adds 180 spaces, 20 EV chargers, LED lighting, permeable paving with attenuation crates, a new barrier-controlled access off the internal road and replacement tree planting. The hospital runs 24/7 so the access road must stay open. Several mature trees will be lost and the site has old drainage runs. The trust has £1.6m including fees and VAT from its capital plan and needs the spaces before winter 2027 pressures.
