# RF-3 — Reconfiguration of a GP health centre, Cardiff

## Story
An NHS-aligned primary care partnership leases a small, purpose-built health centre in Cardiff, completed in the early 2000s and now due a CQC-driven service review. The current layout wastes space on an oversized waiting area while clinicians are queuing for only two consulting rooms; the partnership wants the building reconfigured into more consulting and treatment space, with clinical fittings, a nurse call system and medical gas outlets brought up to current standard. The clinic cannot close — all work has to happen around live patient sessions — and the funding case to NHS Wales is still being built, so no fixed budget exists yet.
