# Review — RF-3-healthcare

Verdict: Mostly well-constructed and internally consistent (programme, risk register, region, budget-absent handling and cost arithmetic all check out), but it fails a top-priority checklist item — the Financial Case section is missing entirely even though Q5.1 gave a financial benefit — and repeats the standards-not-reflected gap seen in the other two test reports.
