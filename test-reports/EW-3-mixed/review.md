# Review — EW-3-mixed

Verdict: The most serious report of the three — the BCIS region is wrong for the exact postcode the brief itself calls out as an example (BS1 = Bristol), which likely understates the cost estimate and undermines the "sufficient" budget verdict, and the Financial Case section is missing entirely despite a financial benefit being given.
