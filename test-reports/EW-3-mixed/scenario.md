# EW-3 — Public realm and car park improvements

## Story
Old Market Quarter is a town-centre mixed-use scheme — ground-floor retail with apartments above — fronting onto a conservation area next to a listed building. The private car park and the pedestrian route through it look tired, the resident cycle storage is inadequate, and a live planning condition from the original consent requires biodiversity net gain to be discharged before it can be signed off. The client wants the car park resurfaced, EV charge points added for residents, planting improved, a proper bin store, and secure cycle storage, while keeping the retail units trading and residents living above throughout. Funding hasn't been confirmed yet, and the indicative budget the client has in mind looks tight against the scope described.
