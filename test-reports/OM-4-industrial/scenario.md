# OM-4 — Distribution centre upgrade, Telford (TF)

## Story
A third-party logistics operator leases a 1980s distribution unit on
Telford's Stafford Park estate and needs to reconfigure it for a new
retail client's contract: more racking, a mezzanine pick-office, upgraded
power for handling equipment charging, and solar and battery storage to
meet the client's net-zero delivery clause. Part of the yard sits on
former mineral workings, so made ground is a known issue, and the
existing hardstanding needs breaking out and relaying to take heavier HGV
turning movements. The contract has a fixed go-live date tied to the
client's next peak trading season, and the operator has set a tight
capital budget because the works are being funded from working capital,
not a loan.
