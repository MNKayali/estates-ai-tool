# NB-7 — Seven-storey affordable housing block, Salford

## Story
A housing association is building 56 affordable flats (1- and 2-bed) on a cleared former garage site off Ordsall Lane, Salford. The block is seven storeys (about 21 m to the top floor), so it is a higher-risk building under the Building Safety Act and needs Building Safety Regulator Gateway 2 approval before work starts. The site has made ground from old terraces and a culverted watercourse nearby, so piled foundations are expected. Homes England grant is conditional on a start on site by summer 2027. The board's worry is cost certainty — the budget was set from a 2024 appraisal and build costs have moved since.
