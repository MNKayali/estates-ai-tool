# NB-4 — Bristol Harbourside health and diagnostics hub

## Story
An NHS estates team is building a new two-storey health and diagnostics
hub on a vacant plot at the edge of an existing hospital campus
overlooking Bristol's harbourside, replacing outpatient services
currently scattered across leased space in the city. The site sits
within the adjoining conservation area, and the harbourside location
means a high water table and known underground services to route around.
The wider campus stays open and in use throughout, so the team wants
reassurance the build can be sequenced without disrupting live clinical
services next door, and that a district-heating tie-in to the campus
network is affordable within the confirmed capital grant before the
funding round closes.
