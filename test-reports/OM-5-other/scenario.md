# OM-5 — Georgian coach house to repair café conversion, Bath (BA1)

## Story
A small environmental charity has been gifted a disused Georgian coach
house tucked behind a terrace in central Bath and wants to convert it
into a repair café and community makerspace — a place where people can
bring broken appliances, bikes and furniture to be fixed rather than
thrown away. The building is within the Bath conservation area and has
sat empty and unheated for years, so damp and possible asbestos in old
linings are suspected, and nobody is quite sure of its structural
condition. A heritage-minded grant covers the works comfortably, but the
charity needs a credible feasibility report to unlock the funding and is
conscious the building's age and setting mean consents will take
care and time.
