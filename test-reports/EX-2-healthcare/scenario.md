# EX-2 — Extra consulting rooms at a GP health centre

## Story
An NHS primary care estates lead needs to extend a modern health centre
that is at capacity, adding two extra consulting rooms, an accessible WC
and a small waiting area extension so the practice can register more
patients. The building is a single-storey post-2000 health centre and
stays fully open to patients throughout — there is no option to close for
the works. A capital grant has already been earmarked and is comfortably
more than the estates team expects the job to cost, so their focus is
compliance and design quality rather than squeezing the budget. There is no
fixed completion date, but the works need to avoid disrupting clinics.
