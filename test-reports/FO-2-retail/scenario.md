# FO-2 — Independent shopfit, Leeds high street

## Story
An independent homeware retailer has signed a short lease on a vacant Victorian unit on a Leeds high street conservation area and needs it fit for trading before the Christmas shopping season. The unit has stood empty for over a year; there's visible damp at the rear and a suspicion of asbestos in old ceiling tiles that nobody has surveyed. Money is tight — this is a first shop for the owner, funded mostly by savings — so the brief is a lean, fabric-and-finishes shopfit with new signage and simple retail fit-out, deferring anything not essential to opening day.
