# DM-7 — Demolition of a 1970s ward block on a live hospital site, Newcastle

## Story
An NHS foundation trust is clearing a vacant three-storey 1970s ward block (3,200 m² GIFA) at its Newcastle general hospital to make room for a new diagnostics centre. The block contains asbestos insulation board and lagging, is linked by a first-floor bridge to an occupied outpatients building, and sits beside the main ambulance route. The trust has £1.1m including fees and VAT and needs the site cleared and handed over to the new-build contractor by the end of 2027. Bats were recorded in the roof void in a 2025 survey.
