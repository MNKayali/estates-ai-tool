# NB-5 — Clerkenwell speculative office development

## Story
A commercial developer has cleared a tight City-fringe site in
Clerkenwell, following demolition of a 1960s office block, and wants a
new six-storey speculative office building targeting BREEAM Excellent
and WELL certification to attract institutional tenants. The site shares
a party wall with the building next door along its full depth, ground
contamination from the site's former use is a known risk, and access for
deliveries is constrained by scaffold licensing on a busy street. The
scheme needs to reach Stage 3 before a full planning application can go
in, and the development appraisal has set a construction budget the team
suspects may be too tight for the sustainability ambitions on the table.
