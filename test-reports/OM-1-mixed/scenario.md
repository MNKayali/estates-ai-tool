# OM-1 — Mixed-use refurbishment above a vacant shop, Nottingham (NG1)

## Story
A city-centre landlord owns a three-storey Victorian building in Nottingham
with a vacant ground-floor retail unit and two floors of ageing office space
above, let to a single small tenant on a rolling lease. The landlord wants to
refurbish the whole building into a proper mixed-use scheme — a relet-ready
retail unit and flexible workspace above — to attract better covenants and
rental income. The upper-floor tenant will stay in place through much of the
works, so phasing and noise control matter. Ageing wiring and heating, plus
damp in the rear return, have already put off two prospective retail
tenants, and the landlord wants a credible feasibility figure and programme
before committing capital.
