# Review — OM-1-mixed

Verdict: Accurate and internally consistent — scope, risks, programme and budget all check out against the scenario answers; the one real defect is a mid-point cost figure used for the Financial Case payback that doesn't match the mid-point figure used elsewhere in the same report.

## Issues
| # | Severity | Category | Page | Report says | Expected (scenario) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Cost | 2 & 8 | P.2: "works cost mid-point of £1,134,000" and VAT mid-point "£364,000 VAT at 20% (mid-point, for reference)". P.8 Financial Case: "PROJECT COST (MID) £1,868,000" / "mid-point project cost of £1,868,000" | Every figure should be derived consistently from the same cost calculation | The p.2 figures (£1,134,000 and £364,000) both back-solve to a true pre-rounding project-cost mid-point of ≈£1,822,000 (using the stated −20%/+25% Grade-C band: £1,457,000 low ÷ 0.8 ≈ £1,821,000; £2,278,000 high ÷ 1.25 ≈ £1,822,000). The Financial Case instead uses £1,868,000, which is just the simple average of the rounded low/high totals ((£1,457,000+£2,278,000)/2 = £1,867,500). The two "mid-point" figures differ by ≈£46,000 (2.5%), and the difference changes the stated "10.1 years" simple payback to ≈9.8 years if the other mid-point is used — a figure the client would act on. |
