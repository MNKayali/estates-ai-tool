# FO-1 — Floor re-let fit-out, Manchester city centre office

## Story
A regional asset manager holds a 1990s multi-let office building in central Manchester. The top floor's tenant has just handed back the lease, and the building has a strong viewing lined up on condition the floor is re-let with a modernised, BREEAM-minded fit-out — finishes refreshed and the ageing heating, power and lighting brought up to a lettable standard, without a full services strip-out that would blow the re-letting timeline. Damp has shown at one perimeter wall and the M&E is original to the building. The floor is otherwise part of an occupied, multi-tenant building, so disruption to neighbouring floors has to stay minimal.
