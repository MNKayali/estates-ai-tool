# NB-6 — Edge Hill student accommodation tower, Liverpool

## Story
A university-linked developer is building a new seven-storey purpose-built
student accommodation tower on a former surface car park near the Edge
Hill campus in Liverpool, with cluster flats, a communal servery and gym
space aimed at first-year students. A historic dock wall and mature trees
along the northern boundary are to be retained, so ecology and root
protection are live issues, and the site's history means some ground
risk is unresolved. Because of its height the scheme falls under the
Building Safety Regulator's Gateway 2 process, and with private
funding still being finalised the team has no fixed construction budget
yet — but they do have a hard deadline: rooms must be ready before the
following September's intake.
