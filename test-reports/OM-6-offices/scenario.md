# OM-6 — Former bank branch to flexible office conversion, City of London (EC2)

## Story
A commercial landlord has taken back a vacated bank branch in the City of
London — a mid-century building attached to neighbouring terraces — and
is converting the banking hall and upper floors into flexible Grade A
office space for a professional-services tenant whose lease starts on a
fixed date. The building has old, under-capacity M&E and suspected
asbestos in ceiling tiles and pipe lagging behind panelling from a 1970s
refit, and fire compartmentation needs bringing up to current standard.
Design is already at Stage 3 and a change-of-use application is in hand.
No capital budget has been fixed yet — the landlord is waiting on this
report before committing — but the incoming tenant's start date is not
negotiable.
