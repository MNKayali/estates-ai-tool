# DM-4 — Demolition of a terraced industrial unit, Telford (TF)

## Story
Ironbridge Trading Estate Ltd owns a 1,200 m² single-storey pressed-metal
workshop on a 1980s industrial estate outside Telford, built in a run of
attached units sharing party walls with occupied neighbours either side.
The tenant went into liquidation in early 2026 and the landlord has a
buyer lined up for the cleared plot, but only if the unit comes down
without disturbing the trading businesses next door and before the
buyer's own finance offer expires. An old transformer compound at the
rear is suspected of ground contamination, and the shared party walls
mean the demolition has to be sequenced carefully. The estate's asset
manager has been told to keep costs as tight as possible — there is
little headroom in the disposal budget — while still hitting the
buyer's deadline.
