# Review — NB-3-industrial

Verdict: Region, scope, risks and the financial case's headline figures are largely right, but the programme silently drops the full-planning stage the scenario asked for (and directly contradicts the cost page's own "full planning" rate justification), and the Financial Case section shows two different mid-point cost figures — one of which, not the one printed in the sentence, is what the payback period was actually calculated from.

## Issues
| # | Severity | Category | Page | Report says | Expected (scenario) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Programme | 5, 7 | Programme narrative (p5): "No planning application is assumed; building control approval runs alongside technical design." No "Planning" row appears in the Programme detail table. Cost page (p7): "Developer and project costs 3.5%: the standard rate for full planning (3–4%, use 3.5%)." | Q3.4 Planning consent: Full planning | The programme omits a planning stage entirely and explicitly states none is assumed, while the cost page's percentage justification on the very next page cites "full planning" as the basis for the developer-cost rate — a direct internal contradiction, and a fail against the scenario's Q3.4 answer. The 73-week overrun against target may also be understated as a result. |
| 2 | High | Financial case | 8 | Stat box: "PROJECT COST (MID) £11,452,000." Sentence directly below: "Simple payback on the £11,174,000 total project cost against the £150,000 annual benefit is 76.3 years." | A single, consistent mid-point figure | Two different total-project-cost mid-point figures on the same page: £11,452,000 (a simple average of the low/high range) and £11,174,000 (the "true" midpoint the low/high range is actually built from, consistent with the cover's £8.94m–£13.97m). The payback figure (76.3 years) is £11,452,000 ÷ £150,000 — i.e. calculated from the box figure, not the £11,174,000 figure quoted in the same sentence. |
| 3 | Medium | Risk | 4 | R04/R07 both describe the same ASHP electrical capacity/DNO check; R05/R08 both describe the same Solar PV roof condition/DNO G99 check, in near-identical wording. | — | Duplicate risk register entries — 4 of the 10 rows are really only 2 distinct risks, each stated twice. |
| 4 | Medium | Programme | 8 | Procurement Recommendation credits Traditional Single Stage Tender with serving only "the client's priority for a certain final cost," noting merely that "the 12-week tender period contributes to the programme challenge against the target date." | Q4.4 priorities: Speed (primary), then Fixed/certain final cost | The client's stated primary priority (Speed) is not the one the chosen route actually serves — a single-stage 12-week tender is comparatively slow — and the narrative doesn't clearly flag that trade-off against the stated primary priority, beyond the passing programme-challenge note. |
