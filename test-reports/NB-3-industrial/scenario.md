# NB-3 — Castle Donington distribution warehouse

## Story
A growing third-party logistics operator based near East Midlands
Airport has outgrown its current unit and has agreed terms on a
brownfield plot on a business park for a new distribution warehouse with
racking, dock levellers and a welfare/office block. The site was
previously a vehicle depot, so contamination and buried services are a
known risk, and a low railway bridge nearby restricts the size of
delivery vehicles that can reach the site from one direction. The
operator needs vacant possession of the new building to give notice on
its current lease, wants the roof left solar-ready, and hasn't yet fixed
a capital budget — this report is what they'll take to their board to
get one approved.
