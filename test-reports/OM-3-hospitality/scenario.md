# OM-3 — Hotel and leisure club refresh, Brighton (BN1)

## Story
An independently owned hotel and leisure club on the Brighton seafront,
built in the early 2000s, wants to refurbish its spa, gym and restaurant
wing and extend the roof terrace bar, all while the hotel keeps trading.
Guest reviews have started flagging tired changing rooms and an undersized
gym, and the operator wants the work finished and reopened before next
summer's peak season. The building sits close to mature grounds with bat
roost potential, so ecology will need careful handling. Spec needs to be
high — this is a premium leisure offer competing on guest experience — and
the operator wants speed and minimal disruption to trading above almost
everything else.
