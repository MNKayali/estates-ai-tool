# EX-3 — New residential wing at a 1960s housing block

## Story
A housing association wants to add a six-storey wing to an existing 1960s
residential block to deliver more affordable flats on a constrained urban
site, ahead of bidding for capital grant funding. The existing block stays
partly occupied while the new wing is built and residents are decanted
floor by floor as work reaches them. Given the extension's own height, fire
safety and Building Safety Act compliance are front of mind for the board,
and the team wants the feasibility report to speak to that directly. No
capital budget has been set yet — this report is what the funding bid will
be built around — but there is a hard deadline tied to the grant window.
