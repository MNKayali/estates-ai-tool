# Review — EX-3-residential

Verdict: The cleanest of the three reports — scope, risk register, region, cost build-up and budget handling are all correct and internally consistent — but the Building Safety Act Gateway 2 requirement, though prominent in the risk register, constraints and next steps, never appears as an actual programme stage or milestone.

## Issues

| # | Severity | Category | Page | Report says | Expected (scenario) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Programme | 5 | High-Level Programme stages: Surveys, Ground Investigation, Stage 2–4, Planning, Gateway (client review) ×2, Governance, Tender, Construction ×2, Handover, Float. No Gateway 2 / Building Safety Regulator row. | "A Building Safety Regulator Gateway 2 programme stage, driven by Q1.6 'Yes' (18 m+) on a Residential extension, per the report instructions in Q6.1" | Q6.1 explicitly asked to "highlight Building Safety Act Gateway 2 requirements given the wing's height," and the report does highlight it substantively — R01 is a HIGH risk ("Gateway 2 approval required before construction; determination periods commonly exceed the statutory 12 weeks"), it is a named item in the Constraints Summary, and both the Procurement Recommendation and Next Steps reference it. But the programme itself (the Gantt-style stage list and milestone table on page 5) has no Gateway 2 stage or duration — R01's own mitigation text ("plan Gateway 2 as a programme milestone") reads as an acknowledgement that it isn't currently a milestone. The scenario's expectation of an actual programme stage is not met, even though every other part of the report treats Gateway 2 correctly. |
