# FO-6 — Ground-floor amenity refresh, City fringe mixed-use block

## Story
A modern mixed-use block on the City fringe — retail unit and shared residents'/tenants' lobby on the ground floor, offices and flats above — needs its ground-floor amenity area refreshed: new finishes, a shared lobby WC, better signage and data provision, plus tighter access security after a spate of tailgating incidents. The building stays fully occupied and trading throughout, deliveries are restricted on the narrow City street, and the managing agent hasn't yet decided how the works will be funded, so no budget or programme has been fixed. This is still at concept stage — the client wants a feasibility view before committing to anything.
