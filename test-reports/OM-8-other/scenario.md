# OM-8 — Community sports pavilion rebuild and extension, Truro

## Story
A town council and a grassroots football club want to replace a timber 1970s changing hut with a new 450 m² single-storey sports pavilion and refurbish the adjoining 150 m² brick clubroom (600 m² total). The pavilion has four team changing rooms, officials' rooms, accessible WCs, a small kitchen and a clubroom bar. Funding is a Football Foundation grant plus council money, totalling £1.6m including fees and VAT; the grant requires a basic, robust specification. The site floods in winter at one corner, and there are mature trees along the boundary.
