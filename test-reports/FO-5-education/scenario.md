# FO-5 — Sixth-form science block redesign, Shrewsbury

## Story
A Shrewsbury independent school wants to convert a tired, Victorian teaching block into a purpose-built sixth-form science and sports wing, with new labs, an indoor sports hall and a full reconfiguration of the internal layout to suit. The building sits in a conservation area within the school's historic grounds, backing onto mature trees and playing fields, and asbestos is suspected in the original ceilings. The governors have secured a capital grant contingent on the works completing in time for the next academic year, and want the works timed around term dates so teaching elsewhere on site isn't disrupted.
