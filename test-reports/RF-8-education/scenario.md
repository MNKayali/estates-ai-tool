# RF-8 — Victorian listed school refurbishment, Bath

## Story
An academy trust runs a primary school in a Grade II listed 1880s Board School building in Bath, 1,150 m² over two storeys. The classrooms have tired finishes, inadequate lighting and an old gas heating system that partly fails every winter. The trust has £1.1m from the DfE condition fund to refresh finishes, replace lighting with LED, renew the heating distribution and fix rising damp in the ground-floor walls. Works must happen in the summer holidays and around term time. Listed building consent is needed for the new heating runs and lighting.
