# EX-6 — Rear extension at a City fringe office building

## Story
A commercial landlord in the City fringe wants to add a three-storey rear
extension to a Post-2000 office building in EC1, to deliver additional
lettable floorspace ahead of a lease renewal with its anchor tenant. The
site sits within a conservation area on a constrained street frontage, and
the existing tenant has to vacate temporarily while the works and the
connecting structural openings are carried out. No capital budget has been
fixed yet — the landlord is waiting on the feasibility figures before
committing funding — but the lease commitment means there is a hard
completion date the scheme has to hit, and the design brief is still at an
early, unsettled stage.
