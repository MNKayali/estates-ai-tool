# DM-3 — Demolition of a vacant department store unit, Brighton (BN1)

## Story
A city-centre estate holds a 650 m² former department store unit in
Brighton's conservation-area high street, vacant since the anchor tenant
left in 2024. The unit sits within a Victorian terrace, flanked on both
sides by trading shops that need to stay open throughout. The landlord
wants the building cleared down to a serviced plot ready for a
speculative new-build letting, but is conscious the conservation-area
setting and shared party walls with occupied neighbours will shape both
the planning route and the sequencing. No budget figure has been fixed
yet — the landlord wants an independent feasibility estimate first, to
size the disposal before approaching the bank.
