# FO-4 — Community clinic services renewal, Liverpool

## Story
An NHS community health trust runs a Victorian-era clinic building in Liverpool that has never had a full services renewal — heating, power and fire systems are original to a 1990s refit and now fail routine estates inspections. A CQC compliance deadline is forcing the trust's hand: the building must pass its next inspection with modernised M&E, a proper clinical room, cleanroom-standard treatment space and medical gas outlets, all while the clinic keeps seeing patients in the wings not under construction. Capital is limited — this is coming out of a stretched estates backlog fund, not a dedicated capital scheme — so the budget is tight against what a full systems replacement actually costs.
