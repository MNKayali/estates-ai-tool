# EW-8 — Retail park public realm and access upgrade, Southampton

## Story
The owner of a 1990s retail park on the edge of Southampton wants to upgrade 3,000 m² of tired car park and pedestrian areas in front of the units: break out and relay the main pedestrian walkway in block paving, new raised crossings, LED lighting, planting beds and trees, a cycle shelter for 40 bikes and a widened junction onto the access road. Shops stay open, so works must be phased around trading. The owner thinks it will cost around £650,000 including fees and VAT but has no firm figure, and wants to know if that is realistic.
