# Review — RF-9-offices

Live report: https://www.projento.co.uk/report/cac838ee5be34585 (13 pages)

Verdict: cost is high (£5,420/m² vs £3,300–£4,500) because the per-m² rates for doors, plumbing and partitions come from residential evidence and are applied to an open-plan office. Construction time (49 weeks) is too short for a back-to-frame job with a new façade, so the target looks achievable when it probably isn't. Correctly, no Gateway 2 for offices.
