# RF-9 — Inner London office reposition, EC2

## Story
A property fund owns a vacant 1990s seven-storey office building near Moorgate, 9,000 m² GIFA. Tenants have left and the building is EPC D, so it can no longer be let after 2030 under MEES. The fund wants a full "back to frame" refurbishment: new façade glazing, all-electric heating and cooling, new lifts, reconfigured cores and a new roof terrace, to BREEAM Outstanding and NABERS 5-star. Budget £45m including fees and VAT. The building is in the City, so deliveries are restricted and a highway licence is needed for scaffolding. Lettings agents want completion for spring 2029.
