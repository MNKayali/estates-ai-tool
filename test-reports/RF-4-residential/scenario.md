# RF-4 — Fire safety cladding remediation, London residential tower

## Story
A housing association owns a 22-storey 1960s residential tower on the South Bank, one of several blocks flagged in its post-Grenfell external wall review. A PAS 9980 fire risk appraisal found the existing rainscreen cladding and roof detailing do not meet current standards, and two spalled concrete sections were also picked up during the same survey. Residents remain in occupation throughout — a full decant isn't funded — so the work is envelope-only: new fire-rated cladding, replacement windows, a re-roof and fire-stopping at compartment lines. The board wants a robust cost case to take to the regulator and to its insurer, who has signalled a premium reduction once remediation is confirmed.
