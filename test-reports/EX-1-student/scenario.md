# EX-1 — New wing extension to university halls of residence

## Story
A university estates team is extending an existing 1980s halls of residence
to add badly-needed bed spaces ahead of rising intake numbers. The new wing
connects to the end of the existing block and adds four storeys of student
rooms plus a shared kitchen/social space on each floor. The block stays
occupied by returning students throughout, so work has to be sequenced
around term dates, and the whole job — planning through to handover — must
be finished before the new academic year starts. The estates manager is
worried the fixed completion date is tight once a full planning application
is factored in, and the capital allocation for the wing was set before
detailed design, so cost certainty is the priority.
