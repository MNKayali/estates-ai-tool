# NB-8 — Net-zero two-form-entry primary school, Exeter

## Story
A county council needs a new two-form-entry primary school (420 pupils plus a 52-place nursery) for an urban extension at Monkerton, east Exeter. The site is a greenfield plot handed over by the developer under a Section 106 agreement, with hedgerows on two boundaries and possible bats in the hedgerow trees. The building is two storeys and the council wants it to be net zero carbon in operation with a high-quality learning environment. The school must open for September 2028 — children have already been admitted for that intake, so the deadline is hard.
