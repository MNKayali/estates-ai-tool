# DM-5 — Demolition of a fire-damaged high street shop, Bath (BA)

## Story
A small Georgian-terrace shop unit on a Bath conservation-area high
street was gutted by an electrical fire in early 2026. The landlord,
Milsom Portfolio LLP, has been advised by structural engineers that the
remaining shell is unsafe to retain and must come down, but the unit
sits mid-terrace, sharing party walls with two neighbouring shops that
are still trading. The council has indicated full planning permission
will be needed given the conservation area setting, and design work is
already at developed-design stage to support the application. No
demolition budget has been fixed yet — the landlord is waiting on the
insurance settlement before committing a figure — but wants the site
cleared and made safe as soon as possible, and sees a clear commercial
upside once the plot can be re-let.
