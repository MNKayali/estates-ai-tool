# EW-9 — Primary school playground and MUGA, Swansea

## Story
A Welsh primary school's 1,200 m² tarmac playground is cracked, ponds after rain and floods the hall entrance. The local authority wants to resurface it, add a fenced multi-use games area within it, new soakaway drainage, a small outdoor classroom area with planting and some new perimeter fencing. All work must happen in the six-week summer holiday of 2027 because the playground is the only outdoor space. The council has £220,000 including fees and VAT from its school maintenance budget.
