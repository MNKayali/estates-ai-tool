# DM-1 — Demolition of a redundant warehouse, Doncaster (DN4)

## Story
Wheatley Park Estates owns a 4,200 m² single-storey warehouse on a South
Yorkshire industrial estate, empty since the last tenant's lease lapsed in
2024. A national logistics operator has offered to buy the plot for a new
distribution shed, subject to vacant possession and a clear site by summer
2027. The building dates from the late 1980s, so an asbestos survey is
overdue, and the estate's own environmental file flags historic
transformer oil storage against the north boundary — the buyer's solicitors
have already asked about it. The estates manager needs a costed demolition
package, including service diversions for the shared estate substation
feed, to take to the board alongside the sale.
