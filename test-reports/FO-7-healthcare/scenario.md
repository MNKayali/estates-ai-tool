# FO-7 — Dental clinic fit-out of a former shop, Norwich

## Story
A private dental group has taken a 15-year lease on a vacant 220 m² ground-floor shop unit in Norwich city centre and wants to fit it out as a six-surgery dental clinic with a decontamination room, X-ray room and accessible WC. The shell is bare concrete with capped services. Each surgery needs medical gases, compressed air and suction, good ventilation and cooling, and CQC compliance. The group has budgeted £650,000 including fees and VAT and wants to open quickly to start earning; the landlord's licence for alterations is in hand.
