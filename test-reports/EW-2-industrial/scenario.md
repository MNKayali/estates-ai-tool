# EW-2 — Distribution yard expansion

## Story
Riverside Logistics Park operates a distribution warehouse on a former industrial estate; the building itself is not being touched. The client is expanding onto adjoining land to add a larger HGV yard, more staff car parking and EV charge points ahead of switching part of the delivery fleet to electric, plus a resurfaced shared access road. Legacy contamination from the site's earlier industrial use is a known risk, and the shared access road serves two neighbouring occupiers, so coordination matters. The warehouse runs 24/7 and cannot stop moving goods, so the works must be sequenced without closing the yard. Capital budget for the expansion has already been approved and is generous relative to the likely cost.
