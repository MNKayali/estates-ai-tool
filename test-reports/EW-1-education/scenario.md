# EW-1 — Nursery outdoor play area renewal

## Story
Hillcrest Nursery School occupies a single-storey building on a tight urban site. The outdoor play area's tarmac has cracked and pooled with standing water for two winters running, and a mature ash at the boundary has been flagged as unsafe by a recent tree inspection. The head teacher wants the surfacing renewed, drainage fixed, the ash removed and replaced with new planting, and covered cycle storage added for staff who currently chain bikes to the railings. Everything has to happen in the six-week summer holiday so the play area is ready when children return in September — there is no scope to close it during term time. No budget has been fixed yet; the head teacher is gathering costed options to take to the governors and the local authority.
