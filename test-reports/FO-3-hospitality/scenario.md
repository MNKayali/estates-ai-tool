# FO-3 — Boutique hotel leisure complex, Bristol waterfront

## Story
A hospitality operator has taken a long lease on a large vacant unit on Bristol's waterfront — most recently a big-box retail unit — and wants to reconfigure it into a boutique hotel's leisure wing: a spa, gym and restaurant/bar with full new layout, structure of internal partitions and complete M&E. The building is modern and structurally sound but was never fitted for this use, so everything from partitions to fire strategy needs redesigning around the new plan. The operator is still finalising financing and wants a Stage 0–1 feasibility view before committing capital, so no fixed budget has been set yet. The scheme will likely be delivered in phases to let early-opening areas start trading.
