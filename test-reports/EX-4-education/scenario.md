# EX-4 — SEN classroom extension at a Victorian primary school

## Story
A local authority estates team is extending a Victorian primary school in
Shrewsbury to provide two additional classrooms and a dedicated SEN sensory
room, after a rise in pupils with additional needs outstripped the school's
existing accommodation. The single-storey extension links to the end of the
original 1890s teaching block and includes an accessible WC with Changing
Places facilities. The school stays open throughout — term-time working
restrictions apply, and the playground has to stay usable for pupils. The
capital allocation was set before scheme design and the head teacher has
been told it must be ready for the start of the autumn term, so the estates
team is worried about both the fixed date and whether the budget will
stretch to the accessibility standard they've committed to.
