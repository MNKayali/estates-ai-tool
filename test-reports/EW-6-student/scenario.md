# EW-6 — Myrtle Gardens halls summer grounds works

## Story
Myrtle Gardens is a 340-bed purpose-built student accommodation block near the University of Liverpool campus. The internal courtyard and approach roads are worn out, cycle storage is badly undersized for the number of students who cycle in, the bin store is too small for termly turnover, and the operator wants to add solar PV and EV charging bays as part of its net-zero pledge to the university. Halls stand empty over the summer break, giving a single uninterrupted window to do the work, but everything — courtyard, cycle storage, lighting, bin store and charging — must be finished, tested and signed off before students move in for the new academic year. No capital budget has been fixed; the operator wants costed options to take to its board.
