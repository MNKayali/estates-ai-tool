# Review — RF-7-residential

Live report: https://www.projento.co.uk/report/7feba96adc60413a (11 pages)

Verdict: cost (£1,091/m²) is at the low end of the benchmark, and the phasing, Gateway 2 and risks are handled well. But "7 or more storeys" is treated as exactly 7, and the default heating priced is a gas boiler while the prose talks about heat pumps.

## Benchmark comparison

The prose describes the objective, not the priced scope. (My plan did not change the option, as a user easily wouldn't.) |
