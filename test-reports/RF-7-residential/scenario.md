# RF-7 — Occupied 1960s tower block retrofit, Birmingham

## Story
A city council housing department is retrofitting a 13-storey 1960s concrete tower block in Ladywood, Birmingham — 96 flats, about 6,500 m² GIFA. Residents will stay in their homes. The scope is new fire-safe external wall insulation and windows, replacing the failing communal gas boilers with an air source heat pump system, new risers, fire alarm and emergency lighting, and fire stopping throughout. The block is a higher-risk building, so the works need Building Safety Regulator approval. Work will be done in four phases (stacks of flats) back to back, with two weeks between phases to re-set scaffolding. The budget is £14m including fees and VAT from the Social Housing Decarbonisation Fund and the HRA.
