# DM-2 — Demolition of a former bus depot, Oswestry (SY10)

## Story
A rural parish council has taken on a disused bus depot on the edge of
Oswestry, empty since the operator relocated in the early 2000s. The two
depot sheds are mid-century brick-and-asbestos-sheet structures, built hard
against the flank wall of a neighbouring cottage that is still lived in.
Nobody has surveyed the buildings — the council inherited them with the
site and simply wants them gone so the land can become extra parking for
the adjoining village hall. Funding hasn't been agreed yet; the clerk has
asked for a feasibility figure to take to a funding bid before committing
to anything, and is worried the number will come in above what the parish
can realistically raise on its own.
