# FO-8 — Cat B office fit-out over two floors, Manchester

## Story
A professional services firm is relocating 280 staff to two floors (2,400 m² NIA/GIFA) of a new Grade A building at First Street, Manchester. The landlord has delivered Cat A (raised floors, ceilings, VRF, lighting). The firm needs a Cat B fit-out: meeting rooms, a client suite, a staff café with a tea point, focus rooms, AV and IT, and loose furniture. The lease starts in May 2027 and the old lease ends in October 2027, so there is a hard deadline. The board has approved £3.6m including fees and VAT, and the firm values a fixed price above all.
