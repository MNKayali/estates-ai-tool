# DM-6 — Demolition of a redundant dockside mixed-use block, Plymouth (PL)

## Story
Sutton Harbour Estates plans to redevelop a 2,600 m² former
warehouse-and-office block on Plymouth's waterfront, most recently used
as a mix of storage below and let office space above, now vacant for
over a year. The building backs onto a disused dock basin with mature
buddleia growth and old roof voids that a bat survey has flagged as
having roosting potential, so an ecological appraisal is already in
hand. The estate has secured grant funding towards the wider waterfront
regeneration programme this demolition is the first phase of, and has
budgeted generously for a clean, well-sequenced clearance ahead of the
next phase's planning application.
