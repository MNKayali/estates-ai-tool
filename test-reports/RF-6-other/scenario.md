# RF-6 — Reconfiguration of a listed former corn exchange, Shrewsbury

## Story
A town council owns a Grade II-listed former corn exchange in Shrewsbury town centre, currently used a few evenings a week by a community group and otherwise standing empty. The council wants to reconfigure the ground and first floors into a flexible community hub — bookable meeting rooms, a small café servery and a shared co-working space — to bring in regular income and daytime footfall. The building's historic timber roof and façade must be retained and the works kept sympathetic to its listed status. The council has set aside a healthy capital sum from a heritage grant and local reserves, and is confident the budget covers the works comfortably; there is no fixed deadline, but the council would like the building open again as soon as is practical.
