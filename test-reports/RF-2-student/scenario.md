# RF-2 — Summer refurbishment of student halls, Manchester

## Story
A university's residential team runs a 1980s-built halls-of-residence block near the main campus. Ageing heating, water and fire alarm systems keep triggering call-outs, several en-suite bathrooms are failing, and the block only becomes free of students for eight weeks each summer. The estates team wants a full mechanical and electrical replacement alongside new bathroom pods, done entirely during the summer vacation so the block is ready for the returning cohort in September — money is tight this year, so the brief is to get the essentials done properly rather than gold-plate anything.
