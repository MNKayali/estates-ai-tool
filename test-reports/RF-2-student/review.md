# Review — RF-2-student

Verdict: Good overall — the target-date failure, occupation and cost-tightness story the scenario is built around come through clearly and consistently — but there are three Medium-severity inconsistencies worth fixing: the selected standard never appears anywhere, the report states two different "mid-point" project costs on two different pages, and a HIGH ecology/bat-licensing risk isn't reflected anywhere in the programme numbers.

## Issues
| # | Severity | Category | Page | Report says | Expected (scenario) | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Cost | 7 | "How the percentages were set" / "Cost assumptions" (p.7) name only the location factor, spec level and intervention band | Q2.5 Standards = University design guide | "University design guide" does not appear anywhere in the report — not on the cover, not in Scope, not in the Estimate Basis, and it triggers no fee or narrative reference (unlike BREEAM in a workbook rule). The answer is silently dropped. |
| 2 | Medium | Cost | 2, 8 | p.2: "£86,000 VAT at 20% (mid-point, for reference)" (implies a mid-point of £430,000); p.8 Financial Case: "PROJECT COST (MID) £440,000" | Same total project cost (£344,000–£535,000) throughout | Two different figures are both presented as "the mid-point" of the same £344,000–£535,000 range: £430,000 (implied by the VAT reference, and consistent with the report's own −20%/+25% pre-range base used everywhere else, e.g. the £348,000 construction-cost mid quoted on p.8) versus £440,000 (a simple average of the published low/high, used only on the Financial Case page). The Financial Case's 9.8-year payback is calculated from the £440,000 figure; using the £430,000 figure used everywhere else gives 9.6 years. |
| 3 | Medium | Programme | 4, 5 | R02 (HIGH): "...bat surveys only May–September; licence adds months" and Recommendation 1: "Commission a preliminary ecological appraisal immediately..." | Q3.8 ecology context "may trigger a bat/ecology survey stage" | The risk register treats bat/ecology licensing as a HIGH risk capable of adding months, but the 55-week headline programme (p.5) has no ecology/bat-survey stage or duration allowance anywhere — the Surveys row only lists "Management Survey; Building condition survey" (3 wks). The schedule risk is named but not built into the numbers the client is given. |
| 4 | Low | Prose | 5 | "One client review gateway sit on the critical path before tender." | — | Grammar: singular subject "gateway" takes "sits", not "sit". |
