# Review — NB-1-residential

Verdict: Answers, scope and region carry through correctly and the budget shortfall verdict is right, but the risk register has a wrong figure that contradicts three other mentions of the same shortfall, a missing demolition risk, an implausible "3 kitchens for 2 bungalows" claim, and a legally shaky Party Wall Act justification.
