# NB-1 — Ashgrove Close infill housing, two accessible bungalows

## Story
A housing association in Hull owns a small cleared infill plot on Ashgrove
Close where two old lock-up garages once stood. They want to build two
single-storey, wheelchair-accessible bungalows for households on their
adapted-housing waiting list, using their usual air-source-heat-pump
specification. The site backs onto a hedgerow that borders a neighbour's
garden, so root protection and possible bats are on their minds. Grant
funding is fixed and the development team is worried the scheme won't
stack up once fees and a park-and-turn area for two cars are added — this
is the first job to test whether the numbers work before they commit the
grant.
